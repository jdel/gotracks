# Cookies and browser storage

Last updated: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

## This service sets no cookies

There is no cookie banner here because there is nothing to consent to. We set
no cookies at all — not for analytics, not for advertising, not for tracking.

## What we do store in your browser

Signing in stores two values in your browser's local storage:

- an **access token**, which proves who you are on each request, and
- a **refresh token**, which obtains a new access token when the first expires.

That is the entire list. These are strictly necessary for a service you asked
for — without them you would have to retype your password on every single
request — so under the ePrivacy rules they need no consent. They are not used
to profile you, and they are never sent anywhere except to this service.

Your display preferences (theme, language, time zone, date format) are stored
on the server with your account, not in your browser.

## Third parties

None. The interface loads no scripts, fonts, images or styles from any other
domain, so no third party receives anything from your browser through this
service.

## Clearing it

Signing out removes both tokens from your browser and revokes the session on
the server, so the tokens cannot be used again even if they were copied.
Clearing this site's data in your browser has the same local effect and signs
you out.

## Changes

This policy may change. If you do not agree with a change you can delete your
account at any time from Settings.
